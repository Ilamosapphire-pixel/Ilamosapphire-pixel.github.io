About

Grounded in the work, not the jargon

I'm a Program Officer, Learning and Development at MakeHerSmile Africa, an NGO focused on adolescent girls and young women across Lagos communities. Most of what I do is unglamorous by design: building the mentor handbooks, facilitator directories, curricula, budgets, and timetables that let a programme actually run — and keeping a distributed team of staff, volunteers, and partner organisations pointed in the same direction.

I'm also a Cohort 8 SDG Advocate with SDSN Nigeria, where I've led community advocacy projects and awareness campaigns, and I'm currently pursuing an MSc in International and Sustainable Development to sharpen the theory behind the practice.

My background is in Guidance and Counselling, and it still shows up in how I approach programme design: start with the person, then build the system around them.
